# Refund policy

*Reconciled from `/shipping` on healthyjewellery.com. Review before pasting.*

---

## Returns

We accept returns within **30 days** of delivery. Items must be unworn, undamaged, and
returned in their original packaging.

To start a return, email **hello@healthyjewellery.com** with your order number. We will
send you a **prepaid return label** — returns cost you nothing.

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund is issued to your original
payment method within **5–7 business days**. Original shipping was free, so there is
nothing withheld from your refund.

## Exchanges

We exchange within the same **30 days**:

- **Size swaps** — we send the correct size once we receive your return.
- **Material swaps** — between Grade 23 Titanium, Niobium, and 316L Surgical Steel
  variants of the same style.

A prepaid return label is provided for exchanges too. International exchange shipping
costs may apply.

## What we cannot take back

**Piercing jewellery that has been worn** cannot be returned, for hygiene reasons. This
is the only category we exclude.

## If your order was cancelled by us

If we cancel an order — stock unavailability, a pricing error, a suspected fraudulent
transaction, or a delivery address we cannot resolve — you receive a **full refund** to
your original payment method within **5–7 business days**.

## Your right to cancel (EU/EEA and UK customers)

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have a statutory right to withdraw from
your purchase within **14 days** of receiving it, without giving a reason.

**Our 30-day return window is longer than that and applies to you instead** — you do not
need to give a reason, and the prepaid label is provided either way. The hygiene
exclusion on worn piercing jewellery applies as permitted for sealed goods unsuitable for
return once unsealed.

To exercise either right, email **hello@healthyjewellery.com**. Refunds are issued using
the same payment method you used, at no cost to you.

## Faulty or corroded items

Our materials are chosen not to corrode. **If it corrodes, we replace it — no questions
asked**, outside any time limit. Email us with a photograph.

## Contact

**hello@healthyjewellery.com** — we respond within 24 hours on business days.
