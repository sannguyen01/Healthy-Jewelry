Every product is made from implant-grade metal: **Grade 23 Titanium**, **Niobium**, or

knowledge.

We display colours as accurately as we can, but screens vary. Listed dimensions are
approximate.

## Prices

Prices are shown in the currency displayed at checkout and may change without notice.
**The price shown when you place your order is the price you pay.**

Import duties and taxes are not included — see the shipping policy.

## Orders

Adding an item to your bag is not a purchase. Your order is confirmed when you receive
an order confirmation email from us.

We may cancel an order for:

- stock unavailability after the order was placed;
- a pricing error on the website;
- a suspected fraudulent transaction;
- a delivery address we cannot resolve.

If we cancel, you receive a **full refund** to your original payment method within
**5–7 business days**.

## Payment

We accept the payment methods shown at checkout. Transactions are processed securely by
our payment provider; **we do not store your full card details on our servers.**

## Returns

Our refund policy is a separate document and forms part of these terms. In short: 30
days, unworn, prepaid label, refunded within 5–7 business days. EU, EEA and UK customers
also have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal, which our longer window already covers.

## Corrosion

Our materials are chosen not to corrode. **If it corrodes, we replace it — no questions
asked.**

## Warranties

Beyond the corrosion commitment above and your statutory rights, we make no warranties.
Products are sold as described, on the basis of their stated material specification.

Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be limited.

## Disputes

We aim to resolve any dispute amicably before formal proceedings. Email us first —
we would rather fix it.
