# Shipping policy

*Reconciled from `/shipping` on healthyjewellery.com. Review before pasting.*

---

## Cost

**Free shipping on every order, worldwide.** No minimum order value, no handling fees.

## Delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days. Delivery takes **7–14 business days**
internationally from dispatch.

Times are estimates from the carrier, not guarantees, and customs processing can add to
them.

## Where we ship

We currently ship to: United Arab Emirates, Austria, Australia, Belgium, Canada,
Switzerland, Czechia, Germany, Denmark, Spain, Finland, France, United Kingdom, Hong
Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia, Netherlands, Norway, New
Zealand, Poland, Portugal, Sweden, Singapore, United States, and Vietnam.

If your country is not listed, email us — we can often arrange it.

## Customs, duties and import taxes

International orders may be subject to **customs duties and import taxes** levied by the
destination country. These are set by your government, not by us, and are **the
recipient's responsibility**. We cannot calculate or prepay them, and they are not
included in the price shown at checkout.

## Tracking

You receive a tracking link by email when your order ships. If it has not arrived within
the estimated window, email **hello@healthyjewellery.com** and we will chase the carrier.

## Undeliverable and returned parcels

If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable — an incorrect address, an unclaimed
delivery, or refused customs charges — we will contact you to arrange redelivery or a
refund of the item price.

## Contact

**hello@healthyjewellery.com** — we respond within 24 hours on business days.
