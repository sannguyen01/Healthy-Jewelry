This policy explains what Healthy Jewellery collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you order: your name, email address, delivery and billing address, and phone number if you give one. Payment card details go directly to our payment provider — we never see or store your full card number.

When you browse: if you agree to it, anonymous page views and add-to-bag events. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, and nothing that identifies you. You are asked once, the default is off, and declining changes nothing about how the site works.

When you contact us: whatever you put in the message.

Why

To take and deliver your order, to answer you when you write, to meet our legal obligations, and — with your consent — to understand which pieces people look at.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Shopify hosts our checkout, processes orders, and stores order and customer records.
  • Our payment provider processes the payment.
  • Our shipping carriers receive the delivery address needed to deliver.

Each acts on our instructions or as an independent controller for their own part. Shopify's own handling is described in the Shopify Consumer Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Correspondence until it is no longer useful to either of us. Analytics events carry no identifier, so there is nothing in them to keep or delete.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK you also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your national data protection authority.

Withdrawing analytics consent: clear the site's stored preference in your browser, and you will be asked again.

To exercise any of these, email hello@healthyjewellery.com. We respond within 30 days.

International transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country, including by Shopify. Where data leaves the EEA or the UK we rely on the transfer mechanisms our processors have in place, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The site is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any revised version here and update the date below.
